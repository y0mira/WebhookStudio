# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and protect private information. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants whose conduct is harmful. Report conduct concerns privately using the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`; maintainers will review reports confidentially and respond proportionately.
